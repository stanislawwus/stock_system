## Notes per component

### collectors

- **Metric**: `price_session_completeness`, the mean over the 77-name universe of the share of
  exchange sessions (warmup + train + validation) that have a daily bar.
- **Gold set**: derived. The exchange calendar is SPY's sessions; the weekdays without a session
  are listed in `docs/02_data_sources.md` and must be exactly the NYSE holidays. Alongside the
  metric the eval reports duplicate share, availability-rule violations and test-period rows,
  each of which must be 0.
- **Why**: a missing bar silently shifts every rolling window and return a strategy computes.
- **Planted errors**: six corruptions of a real validation slice (news row moved into the test
  period, news stamped before publication, bar stamped before the close, a missing session, a
  duplicate article, a +50 % spike). The quality checks must flag all six; the count is in
  `details.planted_errors`.
