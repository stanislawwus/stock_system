## Caveats

- 64 sessions is short: every CI above is wide, and one regime (summer 2026) dominates. Buy-and-hold on a 10-name watchlist chosen with hindsight is a strong, survivorship-flattered benchmark (L-07).
- `impact` and `aggregate_equal` chose their calibrator and hold/threshold on val_a; their full-validation Sharpe is partly in-sample. The val_b column is the honest one.
- Random controls run without the risk manager (the stacked strategies run with it), so a stop that cut a losing trip short is kept as a shorter trip, not as a rule.
- The masking experiment (LLM memory check) was not run in this session.
