# 04 — Data quality

Generated by `uv run python -m src.eda.quality` (git `d7dd902`); numbers are recomputed on every run, the
rules below are enforced by `tests/nlp/test_clean.py`, `tests/test_clock.py`,
`tests/eda/test_quality_audit.py`. CRISP-DM phase 2–3 (data understanding / preparation).
Scope: development data only (warmup, train, validation); every query is cut at `2026-09-01T13:00:00+00:00`, the
first test cutoff, so the test period is not read (R3). Thresholds: `config/data_quality.yaml`.

## 1. Duplicates

| Check | Value |
|---|---|
| news rows / distinct articles | 317,416 / 194,582 |
| duplicate `(id, ticker)` rows (primary key) | 0 |
| articles tagged with more than one ticker | 62,351 (32.04%) |
| rows sharing a normalised headline with another row of the same ticker | 17,779 |
| of those, repeats within 24 h under a different id (syndicated copies) | 418 (0.13%) |

Why it matters: counting a syndicated story five times inflates a ticker's sentiment index and the
"news per day" attention measure. `dedup_key` lower-cases, folds no-break spaces (NFKC) and drops
punctuation, so "Apple: up 3%" and "apple up 3" collide while "up 4%" does not.

## 3. UTC normalisation and conversion to the ET open

- Columns that are not `TIMESTAMPTZ`: 0 (must be 0). DuckDB hands values back in the
  *session* zone (this machine returned `Europe/Kyiv`), so every reader sets `TimeZone = 'UTC'`
  (`MarketView`, `src.eda.data.open_dev`) and every comparison goes through `clock.to_utc`.
  UTC offsets seen after normalisation: 0:00:00.
- News range: 2025-11-18 00:00:15+00:00 .. 2026-08-31 23:53:51+00:00.
- Cutoff of day D = 09:00 America/New_York, i.e. 13:00 UTC in summer time and 14:00 UTC in winter
  time (`tests/test_clock.py` pins both and the weeks of the clock changes).
- Publication phase (New York time): pre-open 43.01% · session 38.67% ·
  after close 18.32%; published on a weekend: 9.56%.
- Decision day of a news item = first session whose cutoff is ≥ its `available_at`. Days between the
  New York publication date and that session: 0 d: 34.05%, 1 d: 49.94%, 2 d: 4.86%, 3 d: 9.48%, 4 d: 1.67%.
  Only 34.05% of articles can be acted on at the open of the day they appear, because
  most are published after 09:00 ET; the rest wait for the next open. Items with no later session in the
  data: 496 (the last development day).

## 5. Non-English text

`clean.is_probably_english`: the share of letters in Latin script must be ≥ 90 %, and for Latin-script
languages at least two distinct foreign function words must outnumber the English ones. It is a
heuristic, not a language identifier; its false positives are listed in the examples above and
checked by hand before a labelling sheet is built.

## 6. Ticker mapping

- News tickers outside the universe: none; universe names without any news:
  none.
- 7,363 articles are tagged with ≥ 5 tickers (15.20% of rows): market
  roundups and lists, not company news.
- The ticker symbol appears as a whole word in the headline or summary for 19.19% of rows
  (lowest: NVDA 6.50%, GOOGL 7.25%, INTC 7.75%, AMZN 10.17%, MSFT 10.30%). Single-letter symbols (C, T, V) match ordinary words, so the
  rate is an upper bound for them; company-name matching is a relevance-component task.
- Filings are mapped by CIK, never by text: 0 of 22,485 have a CIK outside their
  ticker's `cik` / `former_ciks`.

## 7. 8-K item headers

2,670 8-K texts: 1,178 contain at least one `Item x.xx` heading and 2,670 start
with the collector's `[EX-99.1] ...` tag. After `strip_8k_item_headers` 0 headings and
0 item numbers remain. Why: the item number is the gold label (D-10); a
classifier that reads "Item 5.02 Departure of Directors" is reading the answer.
